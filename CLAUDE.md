# Vocabulary Game

## Product

Educational vocabulary game for Brazilian students.

## Language

UI: Brazilian Portuguese

Vocabulary: English

## Core gameplay

Players associate English vocabulary cards with categories.

## Important interaction

Long press on a vocabulary card displays its Portuguese meaning.

Long press must coexist with drag and drop.

## Design

Mobile first.

Game-like interface.

Do not copy existing commercial games.

## Important rule

Do not rewrite working gameplay unless necessary.
